Lead Actors

The Shawshank Redemption

Tim Robbins and Morgan Freeman play the main roles in this movie. Tim Robbins plays Andy Dufresne, a man who is wrongly sent to prison. Morgan Freeman plays Red, a wise prisoner who helps Andy survive. Their friendship is one of the most important parts of the movie, showing hope, patience, and trust over many years.


The Godfather

Marlon Brando plays Vito Corleone, the powerful head of a mafia family. Al Pacino plays his son, Michael Corleone, who slowly becomes involved in the family business. Their performances are very famous and show the change of Michael from a normal man into a strong and serious leader.


The Dark Knight

Christian Bale plays Batman, the hero of Gotham City. Heath Ledger plays the Joker, a dangerous villain who creates chaos. Heath Ledger’s performance is especially famous because he made the Joker very unique, scary, and unforgettable.


The Godfather Part II

Al Pacino returns as Michael Corleone, now leading the family. Robert De Niro plays a young Vito Corleone, showing his early life. The movie shows both past and present, helping viewers understand how the family became so powerful.


12 Angry Men

Henry Fonda plays Juror 8, a calm and thoughtful man who questions the quick decision of the other jurors. He tries to make sure the final decision is fair and based on facts. His role shows the importance of thinking carefully and listening to others.
